# Middleman — design direction

## Character
Editorial, tactile, monochrome, deliberate, contractual.

## References and interpretation
- Light Axial reference: quiet compact navigation, breathing room, reaching hands. Its outer poster's serif headline is not the app type direction.
- Dark particle-human reference: immersive artwork, radically spare composition, typography integrated into the scene.
- Qintara reference: opposing human/technical hands converge on one intermediary. Keep the relationship; reject its purple glow, rounded frame and pill treatment.

## System
- Black #000, white #FFF, one secondary gray #8B8B8B. No gradients, glows, glass, shadows or status dots.
- Inter Tight regular/medium for the sharp grotesk headline, IBM Plex Mono regular for labels, ASCII and contract states. Locally packaged font files, two families.
- Left-aligned two-line poster headline with compact supporting copy to the right. The composition opens into a full-width ASCII handshake around a vault, not a hero-card split.
- 64px desktop gutters at 1440px, 1px rules, square edges. Narrow layout stacks copy above the enlarged illustration.
- ASCII hands are original vector silhouettes filled with deterministic monospace characters. Wallet labels point to a [ MIDDLEMAN ] vault. Moving +/$ glyphs communicate direction, never fabricated transactions. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Below the fold: three horizontal process rows, a concise risk note, footer. No testimonials, metrics, FAQ grid or filler.
